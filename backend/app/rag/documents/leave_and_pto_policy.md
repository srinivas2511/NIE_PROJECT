# Leave and PTO Policy

**Paid Time Off (PTO)**: Full-time employees accrue 18 days of PTO per year during their first
two years of employment, increasing to 23 days after two years and 28 days after five years.
PTO accrues monthly and unused PTO may be carried over up to a maximum of 10 days into the next
calendar year; any excess is forfeited.

**New employees**: PTO accrual begins on the employee's start date, but new hires may not take
PTO during their first 90 days except for approved medical or family emergencies.

**Sick leave**: Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO, which do not
carry over and reset each January 1st. A doctor's note is required for sick leave lasting more
than 3 consecutive days.

**Parental leave**: Eligible employees receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the
birth, adoption, or fostering of a child, in addition to any applicable PTO or sick leave.

**Requesting leave**: PTO requests should be submitted at least 5 business days in advance through
the HR portal and require manager approval. Requests during the last two weeks of a fiscal
quarter may be denied due to business needs.
