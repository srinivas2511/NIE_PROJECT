# IT Security Policy

All employees must follow these security requirements when accessing company systems:

**Passwords**: Minimum 12 characters, including at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one
symbol. Passwords must be rotated every 90 days and may not be reused for the last 5 passwords.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all accounts with access to production systems
or customer data.

**Devices**: Only company-managed laptops may connect to internal systems. Personal devices may
access email and chat only through approved mobile apps with remote-wipe capability enabled.

**Data handling**: Confidential and Restricted data (as classified in the Data Classification
Standard) must never be stored on local drives, personal cloud storage, or removable media.
Use only company-approved cloud storage with encryption at rest.

**Incident reporting**: Suspected security incidents (phishing emails, lost devices, unauthorized
access) must be reported to security@company.internal within 1 hour of discovery. Do not attempt
to investigate a suspected breach yourself.

**Access reviews**: Role-based access to systems is reviewed quarterly by the Security team.
Access not used in 90 days is automatically revoked and must be re-requested through IT.
