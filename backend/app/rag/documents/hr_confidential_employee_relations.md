# HR Confidential: Employee Relations & Disciplinary Policy

This document is restricted to HR and Administrator roles. It governs how employee relations
cases (complaints, grievances, disciplinary actions) are handled.

**Intake**: All employee relations complaints must be logged in the confidential HR case system
within 24 hours of receipt. Complaints involving a direct manager must be escalated to that
manager's skip-level and never investigated by the manager themselves.

**Confidentiality**: Details of an open case (complainant identity, allegations, evidence) may be
shared only with HR staff directly assigned to the case, Legal (if involved), and the employee's
manager on a strict need-to-know basis. Case details must never be discussed outside these
channels, including in casual conversation with the employee's team.

**Disciplinary steps**: The standard progression is verbal warning, written warning, final
written warning, then termination, though severe violations (harassment, safety violations,
theft) may skip directly to termination with VP HR and Legal sign-off.

**Investigation timeline**: Investigations should conclude within 15 business days. Both parties
must be informed of the outcome in writing. Retaliation against a complainant is grounds for
immediate termination of the retaliating employee, regardless of seniority.

**Records retention**: Case files are retained for 7 years and are never visible to the
subject's future managers without HR approval, even after the case is closed.
