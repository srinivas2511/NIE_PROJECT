# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees may be reimbursed for reasonable, business-related expenses incurred while performing
their job duties.

**Submission**: Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date, with an
itemized receipt attached for any single expense over $25. Reports submitted after 60 days will
not be reimbursed except in exceptional circumstances approved by Finance.

**Travel**: Economy-class airfare is standard; business class requires VP approval for flights
over 6 hours. Hotel expenses are capped at $250/night in major metro areas and $150/night
elsewhere, unless attending a conference at a specific venue.

**Meals**: Daily meal allowance while traveling is $75, itemized receipts not required under $25
per meal. Client meals are reimbursable at reasonable cost with the client's name and business
purpose noted.

**Non-reimbursable items**: Alcohol (except at approved client entertainment events), personal
entertainment, traffic fines, and first-class upgrades are not reimbursable.

**Approval**: Expenses under $500 require manager approval only. Expenses of $500 or more require
both manager and Finance approval before reimbursement is processed.
