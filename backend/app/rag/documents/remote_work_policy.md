# Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager approval. Employees whose
role is designated "remote-first" may work remotely full-time.

To work remotely, employees must:
- Maintain a reliable internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps download).
- Be reachable via company chat and email during core hours, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.
- Attend all meetings marked "in-person required" at the office in person.
- Use only company-issued or company-approved devices to access enterprise systems.

Requests for full-time remote work must be submitted to HR at least two weeks in advance and are
reviewed on a case-by-case basis considering role requirements and team needs. Remote work
arrangements are reviewed annually and may be revoked if performance or availability standards are
not met.

Employees working internationally for more than 30 consecutive days must notify HR and Legal in
advance, as this may have tax and visa implications for both the employee and the company.
