# New Employee Onboarding Guide

**Before day one**: IT provisions a company laptop and email account, and HR sends a welcome
packet with benefits enrollment instructions. New hires should complete benefits enrollment
within their first 30 days, or they will be automatically enrolled in the default health plan.

**Day one**: New employees attend a 2-hour orientation covering company values, org structure,
and a security/compliance briefing. Employees receive their building access badge and set up
multi-factor authentication on their accounts during orientation.

**First week**: Employees are assigned a peer "onboarding buddy" for their first 30 days and meet
individually with their manager to review the 30-60-90 day plan. IT training on internal tools
(ticketing system, chat, documentation wiki) is completed by end of week one.

**First 90 days**: New employees complete all mandatory compliance training (security awareness,
code of conduct, anti-harassment) within the first 30 days. A formal 90-day check-in with the
manager and HR reviews performance against the 30-60-90 day plan and confirms successful
completion of the introductory period.
