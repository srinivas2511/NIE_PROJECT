# Executive Compensation Policy (Admin Only)

This document is restricted to the Administrator role. It covers compensation structure for
VP-level and above.

**Base salary bands**: VP roles are banded $220,000-$310,000; SVP roles $310,000-$420,000;
C-suite roles are set individually by the Compensation Committee, not by this band structure.

**Equity**: VPs receive an initial grant of 0.05%-0.15% of fully diluted shares, vesting over 4
years with a 1-year cliff. SVP and above grants are approved case-by-case by the Board
Compensation Committee.

**Annual bonus target**: 30% of base salary for VPs, 40% for SVPs, targeted to company-wide
revenue and profitability goals set at the start of the fiscal year.

**Severance**: Executives at VP level and above are entitled to 6 months of base salary plus
prorated bonus upon involuntary termination without cause, contingent on signing a standard
separation and non-disparagement agreement.

**Disclosure**: Individual executive compensation figures must never be shared outside the
Compensation Committee, the CEO, and CHRO, and are exempt from the standard pay-transparency
practices applied to non-executive roles.
